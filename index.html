DialyCare

Dialysis Scheduling & Notification Application

Smarter Scheduling, Timely Care.

DialyCare is a dialysis scheduling and notification application designed to improve the management of dialysis schedules and communication between dialysis-center staff, patients, and their designated relatives or companions.

Coordination Workflow

  1. Patient scheduling
  2. Session monitoring
  3. Patient and relative updates
  4. SMS notifications

Project Goal

To support organized dialysis scheduling, timely reminders, and better communication among the people involved in patient care.

NeuroComm Board Converter

Assistive Communication System

A microcontroller-based assistive communication board designed to help people with aphasia express common needs through accessible physical buttons and immediate pre-recorded voice output.

Proposed Components

  • Arduino Uno R3
  • DFPlayer Mini
  • EEPROM
  • MicroSD card
  • Speaker and physical buttons

Proposed Signal Path

  1. Physical button sends an input to Arduino Uno R3.
  2. LED provides feedback to the user.
  3. DFPlayer Mini plays a recorded voice through a speaker.
  4. EEPROM stores event information.
Arduino Embedded Systems Assistive Technology Voice Output

Solar-Powered Subsurface Irrigation

Smart Irrigation Concept for Eggplant Farming

A proposed smart irrigation system that combines solar energy, sensor-based monitoring, automated control, and subsurface water delivery to support more efficient irrigation for eggplant farming.

Key Features

  • Solar energy and power system
  • Soil and environmental monitoring
  • Automated decision and control
  • Subsurface water delivery
  • Water delivery toward the eggplant root zone

Conceptual System Workflow

  1. Solar energy supplies the proposed power system.
  2. Sensors monitor soil and environmental conditions.
  3. A controller evaluates sensor readings.
  4. The irrigation system delivers water below the soil surface.
  5. Water is directed toward the eggplant root zone.
Conceptual System · Not a Validated Build
